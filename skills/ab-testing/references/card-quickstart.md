## Terminal response policy

- `status:rendered`, `response_policy:CARD_ONLY`, `allow_text_fallback:false` is terminal host acknowledgement. STOP after the card: no preamble, recap, data replay, failed-display claim, second render or client-visibility confirmation gate. `display_confirmed:false` remains truthful and must NOT trigger fallback. The runner accepts only documented ToolResult payload/raw data envelope/text-JSON carrying explicit `status:rendered`; a card file, success boolean or completed envelope alone is not acknowledgement. Optional post-ack logging failure cannot revoke the receipt.
- Explicit dependency/import/read/build/render failure permits an honest concise measured fallback. START with the runner's `fallback_lead` sentence (the visual card could not be built or displayed, and the actual reason), then the fallback, preserving the exact stage/error and current/prior/window/coverage, priorities/evidence/next actions. Do not mask actual errors. Dependency failures do not prove a ZIP member is absent or a particular tenant cause.
- `status:unknown`, `response_policy:UNKNOWN_NO_REPLAY`, `allow_text_fallback:false` means delivery is uncertain, NOT failed. No whole-data replay or retry; a short uncertainty notice without duplicating the report is permitted.
- Native denial/refusal/suspension: respect the host decision. Do not retry or route around it.

Successful report CARD ONLY applies to report completion, not prerequisite questions, errors, separate user-requested action confirmations or document-first business context/setup. Screenshot showing a card then failure prose does not identify the original raw runtime shape/cause; envelope/text normalization and post-ack logging are defensive fixes, not a proven incident diagnosis.

The A/B cards are generated, never authored: `scripts/build_page.py page.json <domain-short>` writes `findings-report.json` and `test-report-<rank>.json`; `build_page.py page.json implement <rank> <id> <key> created|existing` writes `implement-report-<rank>.json`.
